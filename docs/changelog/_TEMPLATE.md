## ★ Title

**Symptom** What you actually saw — the error string, the wrong behaviour. Write it
so future-you can find this by searching for the symptom, not the diagnosis.

**Cause** The real reason, once known.

**Fix** What was done, and what was *rejected* and why. Dead ends belong here — they
are the most expensive thing to rediscover.

**Verified** The command run and its result. Never "should work".
